Research

AI that can be trusted, examined, and used responsibly.

Dr. Bukhari’s research connects trustworthy artificial intelligence, health data science, biomedical informatics, knowledge representation, and information retrieval.

Research pillars

A human-centered approach to high-stakes computation.

The research agenda is organized around systems that are technically rigorous, clinically relevant, and transparent about how evidence is transformed into recommendations.

01

Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence

Methods that prioritize explainability, accountability, privacy, fairness, robustness, and auditability across the AI lifecycle.

02

Health AI and Clinical Decision Support

AI systems that help researchers and health professionals interpret complex clinical data without replacing human expertise or responsibility.

03

Biomedical Knowledge Graphs

Graph-based representations that connect medical concepts, evidence, standards, and biological relationships for discovery and explanation.

04

FAIR and Interoperable Data

Semantic authoring, metadata standards, and reusable research infrastructure that improve scientific reproducibility and machine-actionable knowledge.

05

Biomedical Information Retrieval

Search, enrichment, and retrieval methods that make complex biomedical content easier to find, connect, and interpret.

06

Health Data Science

Machine learning and data-science methods for clinical outcomes, medical coding, digital health, and biomedical discovery.

Selected active projects

Research programs with direct biomedical relevance.

A concise public overview of major initiatives, intentionally excluding unpublished proposals, private operational details, and sensitive information.

01

RESPOND: Repurposing Drugs for Respiratory Disorders

A hybrid neuro-symbolic AI initiative that combines machine learning with biomedical knowledge to identify existing medicines that may have new applications for serious respiratory diseases. The system is designed to surface transparent scientific evidence connecting drugs, diseases, genes, and biological pathways.

Trustworthy AI for Medical Coding

Research on accurate and explainable recommendations for large medical coding systems. The work integrates deep learning, knowledge graphs, hierarchy-aware modeling, fairness, privacy, and accountability to support responsible computer-assisted coding.

Semantic Biomedical Content Authoring and Publishing

A socio-technical research program that helps scientists produce structured, ontology-enriched content. It combines intelligent recommendations with expert review to improve findability, interoperability, reuse, and trustworthiness.

Narrative-Driven AI for Clinician Well-Being

An interpretable framework for identifying signals associated with clinician burnout in clinical narratives and healthcare data. The goal is responsible organizational awareness and earlier, evidence-based intervention.

Research method

Technical depth with practical accountability.

Represent

Structure biomedical concepts, standards, evidence, and relationships in forms that both people and machines can understand.

Learn

Develop machine-learning methods that combine data-driven patterns with expert knowledge and domain constraints.

Explain

Surface the evidence, assumptions, and relationships behind model outputs rather than presenting unsupported predictions.

Evaluate

Benchmark systems across accuracy, interoperability, fairness, privacy, robustness, and real-world usefulness.
